## English (U.S.)

**Name** (30): `OpenLeaf Notes`

**Subtitle** (30): `Free handwritten notes`

**Promotional text** (170):
```
No ads, no subscription, no account. Take notes with Apple Pencil, write on your PDFs, and keep everything on your iPad.
```

**Keywords** (100 bytes):
```
notes,notebook,pencil,handwriting,pdf,annotate,student,study,school,sketch,whiteboard,sticky,planner
```

**Description:**
```
OpenLeaf is a free, open-source note-taking app made for students. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases. You don't need an account; your notes stay on your iPad.

NOTEBOOKS
• 8 covers with lined, grid, dotted or blank paper
• A4, A5 and Letter sizes
• Folders, favorites, trash, search and sorting
• Quick notes and a whiteboard

WRITE WITH APPLE PENCIL
• Ballpoint, fountain, pencil and brush pens with adjustable thickness
• Highlighter and lasso to select and move
• Partial eraser or whole-stroke eraser
• Double-tap Apple Pencil to switch to the eraser; squeeze Apple Pencil Pro for a quick menu
• Only the Pencil draws; your finger scrolls and turns pages

PAGES AND PDF
• Import PDFs and write on slides and lecture notes
• Export any notebook as a PDF
• Page overview, drag to reorder, duplicate pages
• Pinch to zoom with sharp paper at every zoom level

AND MORE
• Sticky notes: type or handwrite, move and resize
• Take a photo or add one from your library, then write on it
• Back up your whole library as a single file to Files or iCloud Drive
• Light and dark themes, English and Turkish

OPEN SOURCE
OpenLeaf's source code is public on GitHub (GPL-3.0). Report bugs, suggest ideas or contribute: github.com/biggypanda/openleaf
```
